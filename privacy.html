Roomio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the Roomio app ("Roomio", "we") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Roomio is developed by Oguzhan Arslan, an individual developer. Contact: roomioai.interiordesigner@yahoo.com.

No sign-up needed

When you open Roomio for the first time, the app creates an anonymous account identified by a random ID. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app.

Information we collect

  • Your preferences: the room type, styles and budget you choose during onboarding, your App Store country and your time zone.
  • How you use the catalog: the products you like or pass on, and basic signals such as how long a card was shown, how many of its photos you viewed and its position in your feed. We use these to show you pieces you are more likely to enjoy.
  • Room photos and designs: the photos of your room you choose to upload, the room type you select, and the designs created from them.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Roomio Plus subscription and its renewal dates. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • Technical data: our service providers process data such as IP addresses and device information to deliver the service and keep it secure.

Roomio does not use your camera or photo library except to take or pick a room photo when you ask it to. We do not access your contacts or location.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your feed and likes, create room designs and keep your designs in My Rooms.
  • To personalize the products you see, based on your preferences and likes.
  • To apply the free and subscription limits (daily swipes, room designs per month).
  • To improve the catalog using aggregated statistics, for example which products are liked most.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that run parts of Roomio for us:

  • Supabase stores your account data, likes, room photos and designs.
  • OpenAI creates the room designs. When you design a room, your room photo, the selected product photos and a text description are sent to OpenAI's API. Under OpenAI's API policies this data is not used to train their models, and OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions using your random account ID and your App Store purchase records.
  • Apple processes payments and subscriptions.

TikTok Shop links

Products in Roomio are sold by third-party sellers on TikTok Shop. When you tap Shop, you leave Roomio and TikTok's own terms and privacy policy apply. These are affiliate links: we may earn a commission when you buy, at no extra cost to you. We do not share your personal information with TikTok.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your preferences, likes, room photos, designs and subscription records in Roomio are deleted right away. If you use Roomio as a guest (without a subscription) and don't open the app for 90 days, your guest account and its room photos and designs are deleted automatically in the same way. We keep only anonymous totals, such as how many people liked a product, which can't be linked back to you. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before they are overwritten. Your purchase history with Apple is kept by Apple.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime in the app: Profile → Delete account. An active subscription must be cancelled separately in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Reset your style preferences in Profile → Retake quiz.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or state privacy laws in the US), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to its use. To make a request, email roomioai.interiordesigner@yahoo.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (contract) and, for personalization and catalog statistics, on the basis of our legitimate interest in making Roomio useful.

Children

Roomio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used Roomio, contact us and we will delete the account.

International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
